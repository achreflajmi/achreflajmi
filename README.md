## About

Software engineer in Berlin. I work on backend systems and the AI features built on top of them — the data model, the background jobs and the guardrails that decide what an AI system is allowed to do.

Most recently I spent six months at **recash** (Munich), a B2B surplus-inventory marketplace, as the only developer on the platform's AI layer. I designed and shipped **SmartPilot**, an autonomous deal engine that scores inventory nightly, proposes price corrections, negotiates with sellers through an AI agent and routes each deal to the buyers most likely to want it — with a hard rule that no price reaches the catalogue without a human decision recorded against it.

Graduated from ESPRIT in September 2026 with a Diplôme d'Ingénieur in computer science (equivalent to a German M.Sc., EQF 7), mention Très Bien.

**Open to backend, full-stack and applied AI roles across Europe.**
